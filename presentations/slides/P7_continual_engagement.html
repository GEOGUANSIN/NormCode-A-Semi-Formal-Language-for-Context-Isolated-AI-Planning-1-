P7 The Solution
Adaptation Happens. Inside the Contract.
Two mechanisms give each worker limited improvisation — without rewriting the contract.
Mechanism 1 ReAct Loop
Within a single step, the worker observes the result of its action and decides whether to act again. The loop runs entirely inside the declared scope — the contract does not change.
UAV example: The scan step receives an ambiguous sensor reading. The worker retries the scan with a different approach, then delivers the sensor reading once confident. The navigate step never sees the retry — only the final deliverable.
Mechanism 2 Distribute Tool coming soon
A worker delegates part of its work to a sub-worker. The sub-worker receives a scoped subset of the current step's inputs, produces a partial result, and returns it. The original worker integrates and completes the deliverable.
UAV example: The navigate step delegates obstacle assessment to a specialist sub-worker. The sub-worker receives only position and sensor data — not the full mission brief. The full mission context never leaves the navigate step's scope.
Both mechanisms are limited improvisation — the worker adapts, but adaptation is bounded by the declared scope. The contract is not rewritten at runtime.

What the conditions mean in practice

Navigation only
Operator requests a survey flight or route execution
uav_navigation.ncds runs — uav_error_check.ncds is skipped entirely
Error check only
Operator requests inspection of a specific asset
uav_error_check.ncds runs — uav_navigation.ncds is skipped entirely
Both
Full mission: navigate to site, then inspect
Both sub-plans run from the same approved plan
The conditions are declared in the contract and assessed by planning_agent — not hardcoded. The same plan structure handles all three cases.

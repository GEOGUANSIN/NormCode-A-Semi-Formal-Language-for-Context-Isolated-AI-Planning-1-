What a Worker Is

One agent or a group of agents
A worker is the entity assigned to execute a step. It can be a single agent or a coordinated group — what matters is that the group acts as one unit toward the step's declared deliverable.
Abilities are explicit, not assumed
The ability pool defines the ceiling. Workers cannot reach outside it regardless of what they reason about. The boundary is structural, not a prompt instruction.
Each workflow run has its own pool
A navigation mission and an error-check mission may configure different ability pools. The same worker type, different operational envelope.

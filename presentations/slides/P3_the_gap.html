What you're really looking for
1. A contract — specifying what work will be done, in what order, with what inputs. Our mature automation expressed in structured form.
2. Agentic capability — a worker who executes each contracted step intelligently, improvises within scope, and learns from past runs.
The contract is not code. The contract is not a drag-and-drop graph.
The contract is a language.
Why neither current approach works
— Agents: no contract — the worker does whatever it thinks is right. No plan to review. No checkpoint to audit. No scope to enforce.
— Workflows: no worker — the graph is rigid. When the world doesn't match the graph, execution fails. There is no intelligence to improvise.
The boundary between fixed and adaptive is not explicit in either approach.
That is the gap NormCode closes.
